First-Party Cookies

These are generally placed on your computer or device by the website you are visiting. For example, we may use a first-party cookie to improve Site security.

Third-Party Cookies

These are placed on your computer or device by a source other than the website you are visiting in order to enable third-party features such as advertising, analytics, videos, or interactive content.

4.2 Essential or Strictly Necessary Cookies

These cookies are necessary for the Site to function and cannot be switched off in our systems. They are usually set in response to actions taken by you such as logging in, setting privacy preferences, or filling forms.

These non-essential cookies help the Site perform and function as designed, including showing videos, chat sessions, and recognizing returning users.

4.4 Analytics Cookies

These cookies track usage of the Site and help us understand visitor behavior, content effectiveness, and improvement opportunities.
