Introduction

That's a budget: a plan for your money. Just so you're clear — budgeting isn't about deprivation or strict rules; it's about creating a plan that reflects your priorities. Good budgets help you reach goals and reduce stress.

More Than Just Numbers

A budget is a plan for your money that balances what you earn with what you spend. It's not about deprivation — it's about making intentional choices so you can afford what matters.

Fun Fact: The word "budget" comes from the old French word for pouch.

Key Components

  • Income: Money coming in (salary, side hustles).
  • Expenses: Money going out (rent, food, bills).
  • Savings: What you set aside for goals and emergencies.
