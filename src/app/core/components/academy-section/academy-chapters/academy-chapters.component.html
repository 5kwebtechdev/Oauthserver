Introduction

Think of a budget like a GPS for your money. Just as you’d plan a road trip to avoid getting lost, a budget helps you navigate spending, saving, and surprises. Forget spreadsheets – this is about freedom, not restriction.

More Than Just Numbers

A budget is a plan for your money that balances what you earn with what you spend. It’s not about deprivation – it’s about making intentional choices so you can afford what truly matters.

Fun Fact: The word ‘budget’ comes from ‘bougette’ (Old French for ‘little bag’).

Key Components

Every budget has three pillars:

  1. Income: Money coming in (salary, side hustles).
  2. Expenses: Money going out (rent, Netflix).
  3. Goals: What you’re saving for (emergency fund, vacation).
