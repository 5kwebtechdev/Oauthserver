1

2

After signup, your parent creates a family in the app.

3

4

Enter the family code or scan the QR code in the next screen to join their family.

5

FAQ's

What if my parent doesn't have an account yet?

Ask them to download the app and sign up. Then they can create a family and share the code with you.

How do I get the family code or QR from my parent?

In your parent's app, go to Family settings → Share code/QR. They can show or send it to you.

What if the code or QR doesn't work when I enter it?

Double-check the code, ensure internet is on, and try again. Ask your parent to regenerate if needed.

Can I start using the app without entering a code right away?

You can explore, but linking to your parent's family unlocks the full experience.
